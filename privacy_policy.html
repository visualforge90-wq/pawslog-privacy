PawsLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

PawsLog ("the app") is a pet care and health tracking app. This policy explains what information the app handles. Replace the contact details at the bottom before publishing.

Information stored on your device

PawsLog works offline and does not require an account. The information you enter (pet names, species, breed, birth dates, weights, meals, walks, medication schedules and supply levels, vaccines and vet visits, expenses, recurring care tasks, food stock, grooming and symptom notes) is stored only on your device. We do not receive, upload or have access to it. This information is about your pets, not about you.

Photos, documents and contact details

If you add a pet photo, photos to the pet's timeline, or documents such as vaccination cards, insurance papers or PDFs, the app makes a copy in its private storage on your device. Documents may contain personal details (for example your name or address), so treat any backup file you create as sensitive. If you add details such as a microchip number, your name and phone number, your vet's contact details or care notes, they are also stored only on your device. None of this is uploaded to us.

Location (Paw Trail)

If you use Paw Trail, the app asks for permission to use your device's precise location while a walk is being recorded. It uses location to draw your route and measure distance, including when the app is in the background or the screen is off. A notification stays visible while recording, and location use stops when you finish or discard the walk. Routes are stored only on your device. We do not upload, share or sell them. You can delete a trail inside the app, or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

Backup and restore

PawsLog can save all your data (pets, logs, health records, routes, photos and documents) into a single backup file. The file is created on your device and saved where you choose using Android's file picker, for example your phone's storage or your Google Drive. PawsLog does not upload backups itself and we never receive them. If you choose Google Drive or another cloud service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply to the file. Restoring a backup replaces the data currently in the app.

Separately, if automatic backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. You can control this in your device's system settings.

Things you choose to share

PawsLog can create a Vet PDF summary, a Sitter Pass PDF, Paws Wrapped images, a pet ID card, a collar-tag QR code and a lost-pet poster. These are created on your device and are shared only if you choose to send or print them using Android's share options. The QR code contains the contact details you entered (such as your name, phone number and microchip number) as plain text, so anyone who scans a printed tag or sees a shared poster can read them. Share and print these items only if you are comfortable with that.

Home-screen widget

The optional widget shows your pet's name, the next medication and the last meal and walk on your home screen, and lets you log a meal, walk or dose with one tap. Anyone who can see your home screen can see this information. You can remove the widget at any time.

Reminders and notifications

Medication reminders, vaccine and vet-visit reminders and the optional daily check-in are scheduled and shown locally on your device. The app asks for notification permission and, on some Android versions, permission to schedule exact alarms, solely to deliver the reminders you set. During a Paw Trail walk, it also shows a notification while recording. You can turn each of these off in the app or in your device settings.

Pet Pulse, achievements, the care calendar and the toxic guide

Pet Pulse, the achievements, the expense totals and the care calendar are calculated on your device from the entries you have logged. The "Safe or toxic?" guide is a built-in reference that works offline. Pet Pulse, the care calendar and the toxic guide are general guidance only. They are not a diagnosis and are not veterinary advice. If you think your pet has eaten something harmful, contact your vet or a local animal poison helpline immediately.

Languages

The app is available in English, Arabic, French and Spanish and follows your device language, or the language you choose for the app in Android settings. This does not involve sending any information anywhere.

Purchases (PawsLog Plus)

PawsLog Plus is an optional one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns PawsLog Plus, and remembers the yes/no answer on your device so Plus keeps working offline. If you ask Google for a refund, Plus is switched off the next time the app checks.

Advertising

PawsLog is free and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob. If you buy PawsLog Plus, the app stops loading ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app first shows a consent message from Google (the User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only according to your choice. You can review or change that choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use certain information to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interaction data, and diagnostic information. This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can control ad personalization by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or by opting out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

PawsLog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because your pet data, photos and routes are stored only on your device, you can delete them at any time by deleting entries in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them. Data processed by Google for advertising is subject to Google's controls described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
