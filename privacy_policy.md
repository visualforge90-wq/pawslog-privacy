# PawsLog Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 6, 2026**

PawsLog ("the app") is a pet care and health tracking app. This policy explains what information the app handles. Replace the contact details at the bottom before publishing.

## Information stored on your device

PawsLog works offline and does not require an account. The information you enter (pet names, species, breed, birth dates, weights, meals, walks, medication schedules, grooming and symptom notes) is stored **only on your device**. We do not receive, upload or have access to it.

## Photos and contact details

If you add a pet photo, the app makes a copy in its private storage on your device. If you add details such as a microchip number, your name and phone number, your vet's contact details or care notes, they are also stored only on your device. None of this is uploaded to us.

## Location (Paw Trail)

If you use Paw Trail, the app asks for permission to use your device's **precise location** while a walk is being recorded. It uses location to draw your route and measure distance, including when the app is in the background or the screen is off. A notification stays visible while recording, and location use stops when you finish or discard the walk. Routes are stored **only on your device**. We do not upload, share or sell them. You can delete a trail inside the app, or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

## Things you choose to share

PawsLog can create a Vet PDF summary, a Sitter Pass PDF, Paws Wrapped images, a pet ID card, a collar-tag QR code and a lost-pet poster. These are created on your device and are shared only if you choose to send or print them using Android's share options. The QR code contains the contact details you entered (such as your name, phone number and microchip number) as plain text, so **anyone who scans a printed tag or sees a shared poster can read them**. Share and print these items only if you are comfortable with that.

## Reminders and notifications

Medication reminders are scheduled and shown locally on your device. The app asks for notification permission and, on some Android versions, permission to schedule exact alarms, solely to deliver the reminders you set. During a Paw Trail walk, it also shows a notification while recording.

## Pet Pulse

Pet Pulse compares the entries you have logged to point out changes in your pet's routine. It runs entirely on your device. It is not a diagnosis and is not veterinary advice.

## Advertising

PawsLog is free and supported by ads shown through **Google AdMob**. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use certain information to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interaction data, and diagnostic information. This data is processed by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Learn more at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can control ad personalization by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or by opting out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Children

PawsLog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data deletion

Because your pet data, photos and routes are stored only on your device, you can delete them at any time by deleting entries in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. Data processed by Google for advertising is subject to Google's controls described above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
